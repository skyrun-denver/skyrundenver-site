Privacy

How we handle your information.

Effective November 19, 2026

This notice covers skyrundenver.com, operated by SkyRun Denver, an independently owned and operated SkyRun Vacation Rentals location serving the Denver Metro area. It explains what we collect through this site, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Information you give us. When you request a property evaluation, we collect your name, email, phone number, property city, property type, the rental approach you are considering, your target start, how you heard about us, and anything you add in the notes field.

Information collected automatically. When you visit the site, we and our service providers may collect the page you arrived on, the referring page or campaign (such as UTM tags or a Google click ID), your browser and device type, and general location based on your IP address. Some of this is collected with cookies or similar technologies.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry, evaluate your property, and follow up about management.
  • To understand how owners find us and which pages and campaigns are useful.
  • To measure and improve our advertising, including showing relevant ads to people who visited the site.
  • To keep the site secure and working properly.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the site and our business, such as our website host and form processor (Netlify), our customer relationship management system (HubSpot), analytics and advertising platforms (such as Google and Meta), and SkyRun Vacation Rentals systems used to manage owner relationships. We may also share information when required by law.

Cookies and your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can manage ad personalization through Google Ads Settings and your Facebook or Instagram ad preferences, and you can opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt out browser add on.

If we contact you by email, you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in the message or by replying to ask us to stop.

Access, correction, and deletion

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email denver@skyrun.com or call 720-728-1997. We will respond within a reasonable time and may need to confirm your identity first.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry information for as long as it is useful to respond to you and manage any resulting relationship, and as required for our records. We delete or anonymize it when it is no longer needed.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect the information you share, including encrypted connections to this site. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Other sites

This site links to other sites, including the owner login and SkyRun Vacation Rentals pages. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Children

This site is intended for property owners and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this notice from time to time and will change the effective date above when we do. Questions can go to denver@skyrun.com.

SkyRun Denver is an independently owned and operated SkyRun Vacation Rentals location.
