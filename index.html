Two ways.

Every home is different, and so are the rules in each city. The evaluation starts by working out which path fits yours, and one manager decides with you.

Short stays

Nightly and weekly guests on the major booking sites. Pricing reviewed against comparable Denver homes and moved for events and seasons. The full turn after every stay, run by StayReady.

A fit when the home qualifies for a short-term rental license and you want the higher turnover rhythm.

Monthly stays

Furnished stays of 30 nights or more for traveling professionals, relocations, and families between homes. Set per lease, a mid stay check, fewer turnovers.

A fit when licensing does not fit, or you prefer fewer guests and steadier months.
