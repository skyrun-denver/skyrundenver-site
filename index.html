Two ways.

Every home is different, and so are the rules in each city. The evaluation starts by working out which path fits yours, and one manager decides with you.

Short stays

Nightly and weekly guests on the major booking sites. Pricing reviewed against comparable Denver homes and moved for events and seasons. The full turn after every stay, run by StayReady.

A fit when the home qualifies for a short-term rental license and you want the higher turnover rhythm.

Monthly stays

Furnished stays of 30 nights or more for traveling professionals, relocations, and families between homes. Set per lease, a mid stay check, fewer turnovers.

A fit when licensing does not fit, or you prefer fewer guests and steadier months.

Managed by SkyRun Denver

  • You. The owner relationship, the rental program, the monthly statement, the call when you have a question.
  • Guests. Listings, screening, messaging, pricing, reviews.
  • Money. Monthly owner statements and distribution of net proceeds under the management agreement. Applicable lodging tax coordinated where SkyRun Denver controls the receipts.
  • Rules. Help understanding rental eligibility and local licensing. Licenses and permits stay in your name.

From us.

What the relationship is, in plain words, from the person who runs it.

Hi, I am Sebastien, owner of SkyRun Denver.

I have always believed there is a big difference between getting a property booked and managing it well. Anyone can put a home on Airbnb. The real value comes from knowing how to position it, when to adjust pricing, which reservations make sense, and how to protect the property and the owner's bottom line along the way.

Around Denver, there is no one size fits all strategy. Depending on the property, the local regulations, and what you are trying to accomplish, a short-term rental might be the right play. Or a furnished 30+ day rental might make a whole lot more sense. We look at the market, run the numbers, consider the costs, and help you make an informed decision rather than simply telling you what you want to hear.

Once we have the right strategy, our job is to make the ownership experience easier. We handle the pricing, reservations, guest communication, and ongoing management, with our StayReady partners taking care of the hands on work that keeps the home ready. You will know what is happening with your property, how it is performing, and who to call when something needs attention.

I am not interested in winning your business with an impressive looking revenue projection that does not hold up in the real world. I would rather show you the opportunities, explain the tradeoffs, and build a relationship that makes sense for both of us.

If you are considering professional management, let us have a conversation. Tell me a little about your property and what you are hoping to accomplish. We will figure out whether we are the right fit and what the smartest next move looks like.

Sebastien Guite
Location Owner, SkyRun Vacation Rentals Denver Metro

Six cities.

Where we work, and what each one asks of you.

DenverA short-term rental license covers stays of 29 nights or fewer and requires the home to be the host's primary residence. Stays of 30 nights or more fall under the residential rental property license instead. Reviewed November 12, 2026. [Draft, pending SkyRun Denver sign off]Start in Denver
ArvadaShort-term rentals are licensed by the city, up to three licenses per person at $350 a year. A 2026 proposal would limit owners to one license and add enforcement, with a council hearing still to come. Reviewed November 12, 2026. [Draft, pending SkyRun Denver sign off]Start in Arvada
Wheat RidgeLicensed since 2021. Owner occupied partial home rentals are not capped. Whole home rentals are capped at 2 percent of homes per council district, with waitlists in three of four districts, and a 10 percent lodgers' tax applies. Reviewed November 12, 2026. [Draft, pending SkyRun Denver sign off]Start in Wheat Ridge
LakewoodLicensed since May 2023 and limited to the owner's primary residence, with proof of residence, $500,000 liability coverage with a short-term rental endorsement, a third party inspection, and certified notice to neighbors. $150 new, $75 renewal. Reviewed November 12, 2026. [Draft, pending SkyRun Denver sign off]Start in Lakewood
EnglewoodStays under 30 nights need a license and an annual inspection, and the home must be an owner's primary residence, 185 days a year, unless the board grants a waiver. City sales tax of 3.8 percent and lodger's tax of 2 percent apply. Reviewed November 12, 2026. [Draft, pending SkyRun Denver sign off]Start in Englewood
CentennialEvery short-term rental under 30 nights needs a city license, $150 a year plus a $50 application fee, liability insurance, two off street parking spaces, and occupancy of two per bedroom up to eight. The city pages state no primary residence requirement. Reviewed November 12, 2026. [Draft, pending SkyRun Denver sign off]Start in Centennial
