The record.

Documented after every stay.

Not a monthly summary. The actual checklist, photos, counts, and issues from each turnover, kept on the home's record.

  • Checklist by zone. Every room and outdoor space, marked complete or flagged.
  • Time stamped photos. What the home looked like before the next guest arrived.
  • Issues and who fixed them. What was found, where it was routed, and when it closed.
  • Supply counts. Restocked against your home's par levels, so nothing runs out mid stay.
